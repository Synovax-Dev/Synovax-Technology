From ChatGPT or Claude

  1. Copy the list

    In ChatGPT: Settings, Personalization, Manage memories. In Claude: Settings, Memory. Select what is there and copy it.

  2. Paste it into your vault

    Open the vault, go to Settings, Import, and paste. The vault shows how many memories it found before you import.

  3. Pick a folder and import

    Everything goes into the folder you choose ("About me" by default), encrypted on your device. Each memory keeps "import" as its source.

What it understands

  • A list: one fact per line, with or without bullets (-, *, •) or numbers. Each line becomes a memory; its first sentence becomes the title.
  • Paragraphs: text split by blank lines, one memory per paragraph.
  • A SYNOVAX export: the .json file from Settings, Export. Folders come back by name, and missing folders are made for you.
  • A file: pick a .txt, .md or .json file instead of pasting.

Safe by default

No duplicates

A memory with the same title and text as one already in your vault is skipped.

Hidden instructions left out

Text that looks like it was written to steer an AI stays out unless you tick the box to take it. The vault names the ones it found. See the immune system.

Encrypted before it leaves

Parsing and encryption happen in your browser. The server only ever receives ciphertext.

Memories, not whole chats

Import takes the list of facts an assistant keeps about you, or a SYNOVAX export. It does not read full chat history exports.
