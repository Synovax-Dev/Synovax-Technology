Install it

The extension works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers. Until it is listed in the Chrome Web Store, install it from the file:

  1. Download and unzip

    synovax-extension.zip (about 40 KB). Unzip it somewhere it can stay, such as your Documents folder.

  2. Load it

    Open chrome://extensions (in Edge, edge://extensions), turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and pick the synovax-extension folder.

  3. Connect it to your vault

    In your vault, under Apps, make a key named "Browser extension". Tick no folders, and tick Allow it to suggest memories to my inbox. Paste the key into the extension's settings page, which opens by itself.

Read the code first

It is plain JavaScript, no build step, in extension/ on GitHub. The ZIP is made from that folder by npm run extension, the same way every time.

Three ways to save

A selection

Select text, right-click, Save selection to SYNOVAX. The page's address goes with it.

A page

Right-click anywhere, Save this page or chat to SYNOVAX, and the page's readable text is saved, up to about 100 KB.

A chat

On ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot or Perplexity, the toolbar button offers Chat and saves the conversation on screen.

The toolbar button also lets you change the title and add a note and a folder before saving. Whatever you save waits in your inbox, and nothing is kept until you approve it.

What it can and cannot do

  1. The extensionReads the page you chose, only when you click, and checks your vault's inbox key against the tag your vault made for this key.
  2. The extensionSeals what you saved to that key (ECDH P-256, HKDF, AES-256-GCM), on your computer.
  3. SYNOVAX serverStores the sealed box, which it cannot open, and writes "suggested" to your read log.
  4. Your vaultOpens it in your inbox, where you approve, edit or reject it. Text that looks like instructions for an AI is flagged first.
  • It cannot read your vault. Its key has no folders, and an app may only suggest, never change a memory.
  • It reads a page only when you ask, through the browser's active-tab permission. It does not watch your browsing.
  • It talks only to your vault. synovax.tech and this computer are allowed from the start; any other address asks your permission once.
The key lives in the browser

The app key is kept in the extension's storage in this browser profile. Someone using that profile could send suggestions with it, never read your vault. Revoke it under Apps at any time.
