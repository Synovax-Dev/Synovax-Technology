Add a device by QR code

  1. On a signed-in device

    Settings, Signed-in devices, Add a device with a QR code. A code appears; it works once, for 5 minutes.

  2. On the new device

    Scan it with the camera. Your vault opens in the browser and shows a six-digit number.

  3. Compare and approve

    The signed-in device shows the same number and which device is asking ("Safari on iOS"). If they match, press Approve. The new device opens your vault without your passphrase being typed on it.

  1. Signed-in devicePuts a pairing id and a random secret in the code. The secret sits after the # in the address, a part browsers never send to a server.
  2. New deviceMakes a fresh key pair and sends its public key with an HMAC made from the secret.
  3. SYNOVAX serverPasses it along. Without the secret it can neither swap the key nor fake the HMAC.
  4. Signed-in deviceChecks the HMAC, then seals the vault key for the new device's key (ECDH P-256, AES-256-GCM) with its own HMAC. The server adds a session for the new device.
Show the code only to your own device

Anyone who scans it first, and whose number you then approve, opens your vault. Compare the number before you press Approve, and cancel a code you no longer need.

Share into your vault from any app

Install the vault to your phone's home screen, then use the phone's Share menu in any app (a browser, notes, messages) and pick SYNOVAX. The vault opens with a new memory already filled in: check it, pick a folder and save. It is encrypted on your phone like any memory.

  • The shared text stays on your phone. The vault's service worker catches the share in the browser and moves the text into the part of the address after the #, so it never reaches the server, not even its logs.
  • If the vault is locked, you open it first; the shared text waits in that tab.
  • The share menu works on Android with Chrome, once the vault is installed. iPhones do not let web apps receive shares; there, copy and paste, or use the browser extension on a computer.

Open it anywhere

  1. Go to the vault

    Open synovax.tech/vault/ (or your own server's /vault/) in the browser on that device.

  2. Sign in, or scan a code

    Your vault name and passphrase (and the 6-digit code if two-step sign-in is on), or a QR code from a signed-in device. Your keys are made on that device; nothing secret is sent.

  3. Install it, if you like

    On a phone, use the browser's "Add to Home Screen" (or "Install app"). On a computer, the install button in the address bar. It then opens in its own window, like any app.

A new device needs no old one

Your passphrase is enough on any device. Lost it? Your 24 recovery words open the vault on any device and set a new passphrase.

Every signed-in device

Settings, Signed-in devices lists every place your vault is open, with a label from the browser ("Chrome on Windows", "Safari on iOS"), when it signed in and when it was last active. This device is marked.

  • Sign out any one of them, or all other devices at once.
  • Change your passphrase and every other session is signed out.
  • A session lasts at most 7 days. The server keeps only a hash of its token.

Locked when you leave

Kept in the tab only

The session token and your keys live in the open tab's memory. Closing or reloading the tab locks the vault, also on a device added by QR code.

No cookies

Nothing is left behind in the browser that could open your vault later.

Two-step sign-in

Turn on a code from an authenticator app in Settings. Each code works once.

Your device is part of the lock

While the vault is open, it is decrypted in that browser. A device with malware, or one someone else is using, can see what you see. Lock the vault (close the tab) on shared computers.
