Open it anywhere

  1. Go to the vault

    Open synovax.tech/vault/ (or your own server's /vault/) in the browser on that device.

  2. Sign in

    Your vault name and passphrase, and the 6-digit code if two-step sign-in is on. Your keys are made on that device from your passphrase; nothing secret is sent.

  3. Install it, if you like

    On a phone, use the browser's "Add to Home Screen" (or "Install app"). On a computer, the install button in the address bar. It then opens in its own window, like any app.

A new device needs no old one

There is no pairing step: your passphrase is enough on any device. Lost it? Your 24 recovery words open the vault on any device and set a new passphrase.

Every signed-in device

Settings, Signed-in devices lists every place your vault is open, with a label from the browser ("Chrome on Windows", "Safari on iOS"), when it signed in and when it was last active. This device is marked.

  • Sign out any one of them, or all other devices at once.
  • Change your passphrase and every other session is signed out.
  • A session lasts at most 7 days. The server keeps only a hash of its token.

Locked when you leave

Kept in the tab only

The session token and your keys live in the open tab's memory. Closing or reloading the tab locks the vault.

No cookies

Nothing is left behind in the browser that could open your vault later.

Two-step sign-in

Turn on a code from an authenticator app in Settings. Each code works once.

Your device is part of the lock

While the vault is open, it is decrypted in that browser. A device with malware, or one someone else is using, can see what you see. Lock the vault (close the tab) on shared computers.
