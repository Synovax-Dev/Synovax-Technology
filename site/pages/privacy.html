The short version

  • Everything you write, import or name in your vault is encrypted on your device before it is sent. The server stores ciphertext.
  • The server does keep some metadata in readable form, listed in full below, because it cannot work without it.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party scripts, and no cookies.
  • You can export everything and delete everything at any time, from your vault.
  • SYNOVAX does not sell your data and does not use it to train AI models.

Never stored or seen by the server: your passphrase, the 24 words, your vault key, folder keys and app keys, the titles, text and tags of memories and suggestions, folder and app names, what you or an app searched for, and the search index the connector builds on your computer.

Cookies and your browser

SYNOVAX sets no cookies. The vault keeps your session token in the open tab's memory only; closing or reloading the tab locks the vault. In your browser's own storage, the vault keeps the newest seal it has seen (a number and a hash), to check that the seal history was not rewritten. If you connect a wallet on the home page, it remembers which wallet extension you picked, so it can reconnect; disconnecting forgets it. The admin panel keeps its session for the open tab only.

Connected AI apps

When you connect an app, the SYNOVAX connector on your computer decrypts the folders you granted and hands their text to that app. From that moment, the app and its provider process that text under their own privacy policies. SYNOVAX records the read in your log but has no control over what the app does next.

If you turn on search by meaning in the connector, a model runs on your computer. It is downloaded once from Hugging Face; your memories are never sent to it or anywhere else.

The $SYNOVAX section

Connecting a wallet on the home page is read only: the page asks your wallet extension for your address and reads your token balance from Robinhood Chain through the wallet. Nothing about it is sent to the SYNOVAX server. The top holders list is counted by the server from public chain data.

Why it is kept

The data above is processed to provide what you asked for: storing your vault, signing you in, letting the apps you choose read it, and showing you who read what. The short-lived rate limit entries protect the service and its users from abuse. Nothing is used for anything else.

How long it is kept

  • Your vault, folders, memories, apps and read log: until you delete them, or your account.
  • Sessions: 7 days, or until you sign out. Suggestions: until you approve or reject them.
  • Rate limit entries: one hour.
  • Database backups: by default a copy every 6 hours, the newest 14 kept, so deleted data can remain in them for about three and a half days.
  • Seals: permanently, as a public record. They are built from salted hashes, and your salts are deleted with your account, after which nothing in a seal can be linked to you.

What you can do

  • See and take everything: Settings, Export downloads every memory, decrypted, as one JSON file.
  • Correct: edit any memory, folder or app in your vault.
  • Delete: memories, folders, apps or your whole account, in your vault. Deletion removes the data from the database and overwrites it on disk.
  • Stop a use: revoke any app, sign out any session.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Where the data is, and contact

The data is held by the server that runs SYNOVAX and by its hosting provider. The web server in front of it keeps standard access logs (network address, time, address requested) as part of normal operation. Anyone can run their own SYNOVAX server from the open source code; then that server's operator holds the data instead.

Questions or requests can be sent through the repository or the SYNOVAX account on X; please do not post personal details publicly. If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
