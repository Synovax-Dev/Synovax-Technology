SYNOVAX

Connect to your vault

The extension sends what you save to your vault's inbox, sealed so only your vault can open it. It cannot read your vault.

  1. Open your vault and go to Apps.
  2. Name the app "Browser extension", tick no folders, and tick Allow it to suggest memories to my inbox.
  3. Press Create app key and paste the key below.

The key is kept in this browser's extension storage, on this computer. Anyone who can use this browser profile can send suggestions with it, never read your vault. Revoke it under Apps at any time.
