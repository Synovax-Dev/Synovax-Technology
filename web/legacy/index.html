Product · legacy

Who receives your vault,and only when

Name up to three people whose vaults receive yours if something happens to you. They get every folder and memory, read only, and only after you have not opened your vault for the time you choose.

Name an heir

  1. Get their share code

    They find it in their vault, under Shared. Like sharing a folder, the code lets your vault check that the server hands over their real key.

  2. Choose the wait

    In Settings, Legacy, paste the code and pick how long you must stay away first: 30, 90, 180 or 365 days.

  3. That is all

    Your vault seals its key for them, tagged by both your vaults, and the server keeps the sealed box. They see that you named them, under Left to you, but cannot open anything yet.

When it opens

Only after you stay away

Every time you open your vault, its "last seen" time moves. The sealed key goes to your heir only once that time is older than the wait you chose.

Coming back closes it

If you open your vault again, it closes for them at once. A wait that ended by mistake (a long trip, a lost phone) undoes itself.

On the record

Each time an heir opens your vault, that is a line in your read log, sealed with its hour.

Your heir is never told when you were last seen, only whether the vault is open to them now.

What they receive

  • Every folder and memory, read only, decrypted in their browser with your vault key. They can read it there and download it as one file.
  • Not your apps or your inbox. They cannot sign in as you, change anything, or use your app keys.
  • Nothing readable on the way. The server holds your vault key only as a box sealed for their vault (ECDH P-256 and AES-256-GCM), with a tag that only your two vaults can make.
  • You can remove an heir at any time, and an heir can decline.
The server keeps the time

The cryptography stops the server from reading your vault, but the server decides when the wait has passed. A dishonest server working with your heir could open it early, and once your heir has read your vault, they have it. Name only someone you would trust with it anyway.
