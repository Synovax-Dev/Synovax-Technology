Product · sharing

One folder,read by a vault you trust

Share a single folder with a partner, a colleague or a family member's vault. They can read it, now and as it grows, but never change it. You see every time they open it, and you can stop at any moment.

Share a folder

  1. They send you their share code

    In their vault, under Shared, there is a code like synovax-share:name:…. It holds their vault name and a fingerprint of their vault's public key. It is not a secret; it only lets people share with them.

  2. You open the folder and choose Share

    Paste their code and press Share. Your vault fetches their public key, checks it against the fingerprint in the code, and seals the folder's key for them.

  3. They open it under Shared

    The folder appears in their vault, read only, with your vault name and the end of your share code, so they can check it really came from you.

What the server can and cannot do

  1. Your vaultChecks the recipient's key against the fingerprint in their code, then seals the folder key to it (ECDH P-256, HKDF, AES-256-GCM).
  2. Your vaultAdds a tag made from your key and theirs together (static ECDH and HMAC), which only your two vaults can make.
  3. SYNOVAX serverStores the sealed box and passes it on. It cannot open it, and it cannot make one that passes the tag.
  4. Their vaultOpens the box, checks the tag against your public key, then decrypts the memories in their browser.
A swapped key is caught

If the server answered with its own key instead of theirs, the fingerprint would not match and nothing would be shared.

A forged share is refused

A box sealed by anyone but you fails the tag, so the server cannot slip a made-up folder into their vault.

Compare the code once

The one check left to people: the recipient sees the end of your share code and can compare it with you, in person or by phone.

You stay in control

  • Read only. The other vault can read every memory in the folder, including ones you add later, but cannot change or delete anything.
  • On the record. Each time their vault opens the folder, it is a line in your read log, sealed with its hour like any read.
  • Stop at any time. Stop sharing and their vault is cut off at once. The folder then gets a new key, so a copy of the old one opens nothing you write from then on.
  • They can leave. The recipient can remove a shared folder from their vault whenever they like.
  • A folder can be shared with up to 20 vaults. Replacing a shared folder's key seals the new key for each of them.
Sharing is trust

Once the other vault has read a memory, they have it. Stopping a share ends future reads, not past ones. Share only a folder you would be fine with them keeping. Shared folders are read in their vault; their AI apps do not see them.
