Product · inbox

Suggested by your AI,kept only if you say so

An app you allow can suggest a memory while you talk to it. The suggestion is sealed so only your vault can open it, and it waits in your inbox. Nothing is saved until you approve it.

How a suggestion travels

  1. Your AI appCalls suggest_memory with a title, the text, tags, a folder it thinks fits, and why.
  2. Connector, your computerChecks that the inbox key really belongs to your vault, then seals the suggestion to it (ECDH P-256, HKDF and AES-256-GCM).
  3. SYNOVAX serverStores the sealed box, which it cannot open, and writes "suggested" to your read log.
  4. Your vaultOpens the box in your browser and shows it in the inbox, ready to edit.

The connector checks a tag over your inbox key (an HMAC made with a secret from the app key) before it seals anything. A server that swapped in its own key to read suggestions would fail that check.

You decide

  • Approve saves it to the folder you pick, encrypted on your device, with the app's name as its source. Edit the title, text and tags first if you like.
  • Reject deletes it from the server.
  • The vault checks the inbox every 30 seconds while it is open and tells you when something new arrives.
Hidden instructions are flagged

A suggestion that looks like instructions for an AI shows a "looks like instructions for an AI" note with the reasons, before you approve it. See the immune system.

Which apps may suggest

Only apps you allowed. When you make an app key under Apps, tick "Allow it to suggest memories to my inbox"; you can change it later. An app without it gets a refusal.

30 an hour

Each app can send at most 30 suggestions an hour.

500 waiting

The inbox holds up to 500 suggestions at a time. Decide on some and room opens up.

Write only

Suggesting is the only write an app has. No tool lets an app change or delete a memory.
