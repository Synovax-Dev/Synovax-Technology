Product · the vault

One memoryfor every AI

Your vault holds what your AI apps should know about you: memories, in folders, each folder with its own key. It is encrypted in your browser before anything is sent, and every app reads only the folders you tick.

What is in it

Memories

A title, the text, tags, and where it came from (you, an app or an import). Up to 200,000 characters each, and up to 20,000 in a vault.

Folders

"About me", "Work", "Health": up to 200, each with its own random key, so an app given one folder can never open another.

Apps

Every AI app gets its own key and only the folders you tick. Apps covers the setup.

Inbox

Apps you allow can suggest a memory. It waits, sealed, until you approve it. See Inbox.

Read log

Every list, search, read and suggestion by an app, with the folder or memory it touched. Folded into an hourly seal.

Settings

Export, import, two-step sign-in, your devices, a new passphrase, or delete everything.

Writing a memory

Open the vault, pick a folder and write. When you save, your browser encrypts the title, tags and text with that folder's key (AES-256-GCM) and sends only ciphertext. The server stores it without being able to read a word of it.

  • Search in the vault runs in your browser, over memories it has already decrypted. Your search words are never sent.
  • A memory that looks like it carries instructions for an AI (text written to steer a model, hidden or disguised) gets a note with the reasons, so you can fix it. The immune system explains the checks.
  • Short, clear titles and tags help your AI find the right memory: use the words you would ask with.

Folders and their keys

Each folder key is locked with your vault key, which is locked with your passphrase and, separately, with your 24 recovery words. An app gets a copy of only the folder keys you granted, locked for that app.

  • Replace key on a folder makes a new key on your device, re-encrypts every memory in it, and gives the new key only to the apps that still have access.
  • When you take a folder away from an app, or revoke an app, the vault replaces that folder's key for you, so a copy the app kept stops working.
  • Deleting a folder deletes every memory in it. The vault asks first.

How your AI reads it

  1. Your AI appAsks the SYNOVAX connector to search, for example "what should I avoid eating".
  2. Connector, your computerFetches the encrypted heads of the folders this app may read.
  3. SYNOVAX serverChecks the app's token, writes the request to your read log, answers with ciphertext.
  4. Connector, your computerDecrypts with the app key, searches, removes hidden instructions, and hands the results to the app as quoted data.

The connector is one command, npx -y synovax connector, started by your AI app. Connector has every detail.

Yours to take or delete

  • Export downloads every folder and memory, decrypted on your device, as one JSON file. Import takes that file back, folders and all.
  • Delete everything removes your vault, folders, memories, apps and read log from the database.
  • Lost your passphrase? Your 24 recovery words open the vault on any device and set a new one.
Free, and yours to run

Use the hosted vault at synovax.tech/vault/, or run your own server with npx synovax. It is open source under the MIT license.
